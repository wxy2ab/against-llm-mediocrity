# Aggregation Mismatch: Wave34 and Cross-Configuration Probes

Updated: 2026-08-05<br>
Chinese version: [聚合失配：Wave34 与跨配置最小探针](./aggregation-mismatch-wave34-and-cross-configuration-probes.zh-CN.md)

## Verdict

The completed supplements close several specific gaps but do not turn the V1–V25 program into a full cross-model replication. They provide three useful outcomes:

1. answer-information externalization and the gated-ledger package retain the same positive direction on MiniMax;
2. the harder V5b oracle-delivery grid remains ceiling-limited on both DeepSeek and MiniMax, so it cannot adjudicate Patch versus Rewrite;
3. seal-before-drift Intent/Exact experiments can escape the old V13 ceiling, while the first synthetic configuration-domain transfer is promising but remains a pilot.

The writing blind pack did not create independent human confirmation: its same-family LLM panel returned all ties, and human-editor evaluation is still pending.

## What each probe changes

### Answer information transfers better than structural-position specificity

MiniMax reproduced the large A2−A0 recovery direction. It did not establish that structural cut-set positions are better than an equal amount of correct answer information at random positions. The engineering implication is to externalize sufficient decisive information; do not overfit the scaffold to an unconfirmed graph-position mechanism.

### Harder payloads did not rescue the Patch–Rewrite contrast

Both V5b deployments left Patch and Rewrite above 90% success. This is an identification failure, not evidence of practical equivalence. A future crossover study needs a grid that avoids both ceiling and floor while separately measuring plan quality, transport, payload size, edit density, and exact commit success.

### Cross-configuration results are mechanism-specific

The gated-ledger package passed a small MiniMax probe. The V12 sparse Patch/Full probe did not transfer because the two arms failed for different reasons: Patch produced invalid new values, while Full suffered transport interruptions with zero reported tokens. Treating the combined zero rate as “both methods fail equally” would erase the causal information in the failure layers.

### TOCTOU governance can leave the V13 ceiling

V13b changed the timing and recovery policy so Compatible Exact could no longer recover after seal-before-drift. That cell fell to `0/24` while Intent and disjoint controls remained `24/24`. T1 showed the same direction on synthetic configuration fixtures. This supports the state-governance mechanism, but T1 is not evidence about production Kubernetes or feature-flag systems.

### The writing blind pack is not independent confirmation

All 20 same-family panel ratings were ties. That result may reflect judge resolution, source-pair differences, or treatment signal. It does not overwrite V22-r6, V23S, or V25, and it cannot substitute for two independent human editors.

## Engineering implications

- Keep cross-provider enablement at the **mechanism** level, not at the whole-Agent level.
- Store provider capability metadata for reasoning mode, transport reliability, payload limits, and schema adherence.
- Do not retry a floor blindly. Route `precondition` failures to plan/value repair and `transport` failures to client/payload handling.
- Treat ceiling as a design failure for comparative research; move the task window before claiming equality.
- Keep cross-configuration probe results separate from production defaults until a matched full matrix passes.
- Require independent human evaluation before promoting natural-writing quality claims.

## Claim ceiling

These supplements support “same direction in a minimum probe” and “the synthetic mechanism can be made identifiable.” They do not support:

- complete MiniMax replication of V3 or V5–V12;
- a universal Patch/Rewrite crossover;
- production reliability of the T1 fixtures;
- equivalence from the V5b ceiling or V12-B1 floor;
- independent human confirmation of the writing findings.
